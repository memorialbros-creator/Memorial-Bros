Straight pricing

What a driveway actually costs

Most companies make you call to find out. Here's our real pricing — concrete driveway, normal condition, clear access.

Concrete driveway cleaning

$175 minimum on any job
SizeTypicallyPrice
Up to 600 sq ft Small pad, walkway, or a short one-car drive $175
800 sq ft Standard 40 × 20 two-car driveway $210
1,200 sq ft Long two-car or a three-car drive $315
1,600 sq ft Large or estate driveway $390
2,000 sq ft Very large residential driveway $485

Other surfaces adjust from there. Sidewalks and walkways run about 5% more per square foot, patios and pool decks about 20% more — they're slower work with more edges to cut in.

Premium materials cost more because they have to be cleaned slower and gentler:

  • Stamped / sealed concrete +25%
  • Pavers +35%
  • Brick or tile +40%
  • Travertine +50%
  • Flagstone & natural stone +55%

Heavy staining, tight access, or delicate landscaping can move the number. That's why we like a photo first — then the price we send you is the price you pay.
